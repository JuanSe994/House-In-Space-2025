# House-In-Space-2025
This is the House In Space Chat-bot code, you can create all the models about houses in mars, moon and the space.

Starting, there are some 3 flash prompts. you can select ONE of them and start creating your model or spacecraft (spacerafts is only ein the space), after that the chat-bot will ask you the type of the model, if do you want a fixed-surface model, a subterrean model ect.. (this only applies in the models of the moon, and mars), after this, the chat-bot will ask you the second question that is, what material do you want for your spacecraft or the mars/moon model (the chat-bot may give a recomendated materials), then, the chat-bot give the third question, "what shape do you want for your model/spacecraft?", you can select one of the recomendate (like the lasted question) or write the shape that you want, then the chat-bot collects all the previous information and it will ask the last question: "What was the completed project intended to be used for?", In this case, We are participating in the Internacional NASA Space Apps Challenge so we select the challenge that is creating a living places in mars and moon, of course understanding the problems that NASA give us (you can go to the documentation of the proyect for how we made the chat-bot and how we use the NASA information to do this proyect), so the are going to choose the live in the mars (example, aplies in all the types of you create your "home" or "spacecraft"), and finaly the chat-bot recopilyes ALL the user information to create TWO prompts; a high Quality (4K) model of your proyect and a enginering sketch type of the proyect, and then they give the aproximated prices of your proyect and the phases of your proyect.

This is only the chat-bot, you can visit the official page of House In Space to see the information about creating your home, about us and more...
